The Belief Systems Audit — Privacy

Consent version: 2026-08-01

The audit runs entirely in your browser. Your written answers never leave your device — there is no code path that transmits them.

If you choose to, you can send four percentages to help build the research base. Nothing else. That choice is opt-in, it happens after you've seen your result, and you can skip it and keep the full result.

What the audit stores on your device

Everything you do is saved in your browser's local storage so you can close the tab and come back:

  • Your answers to every question, including all written responses and Deep-mode reflections
  • Your progress in an unfinished run
  • Your completed run history — up to the last 24 runs, so you can see change over time

This never goes anywhere. It is on your machine, in your browser, and clearing your browser data deletes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

What is never sent

  • Every word you wrote. All written answers and all Deep-mode reflections. There is no code path from those fields to any server. The submitted payload is built from your scores alone, never from your answers.
  • Your name, unless you put it in the optional email field
  • Your IP address, browser, device, or location
  • Any cookie or tracking identifier
  • Anything at all, if you don't tick the box
You can verify this. After sending, the page shows you the literal, complete data that was transmitted. Not a summary of it — the actual payload. If it isn't in that box, it wasn't sent.

The run ID, and what it deliberately can't do

Each run gets a fresh random ID. It is not tied to you or to your browser.

This is a deliberate limit. A persistent per-browser identifier would let us link your runs together across months and build a profile. We don't want that capability, so we didn't build it. The consequence is that if you take the audit again next month, we cannot tell it's you — and that's the intended trade.

The only exception: if you extend a run (say, 8 questions up to 12) in the same sitting, the ID carries over so your record is updated rather than duplicated.

Where it goes and how long it stays

Submitted scores land in a private Google Sheet controlled by Impart. They are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Aggregate findings — "X% of people in career transition score lowest on Community" — may be published in Impart's writing, talks, and licensing materials. Aggregates only. With no names, no emails, and no written answers in the dataset, individual results are not identifiable in anything published.

If you gave an email, it may be used to send you follow-up about Impart. You can ask to be removed at any time.

Retention: anonymous score records are kept indefinitely as research data. Email addresses are deleted on request.

Deleting your data

Email julianf@fitzfirm.net and ask. If you have your run ID (shown on the results screen after sending), include it and the exact record can be removed. Without it, any email address you gave can still be removed.

To delete what's stored locally, clear your browser data for this site — that is immediate, complete, and doesn't involve us.

What this is not

The Belief Systems Audit is a workforce-development diagnostic. It is not therapy, not a mental health assessment, and not a clinical instrument. It does not diagnose anything.

The framework itself carries this guardrail: trauma and fear install beliefs instantly, bypassing the process this audit measures. Where that applies, healing comes before belief work — and that is a job for a licensed professional, not a questionnaire.

If you are in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or an emergency service in your country.
