Four guardrails, always

1. Echo chambers build limiting beliefs as fast as empowering ones. You need people who challenge you, not only agree.

2. Reflection can lie. Ask outright whether you're cherry-picking evidence.

3. Trauma and fear install beliefs instantly, bypassing all four elements. Healing comes before belief work. This is not therapy.

4. This is a mechanism, not a moral. Used on other people without their interest at heart, it's manipulation.

Where the chain breaks

The Lowest Score Rule. You don't work on your strongest element — you work on your lowest. These four are a conversion chain, not a scorecard to average. Three strong and one weak still produces a belief that doesn't hold, because the weak one is where the conversion breaks.
